# **Changelog of My Life As a Developer in Development**
---
## 2026
- Started using VS Code as the main development environment
- Learned to use GitHub as a development and project management platform
- Started standardizing mod structures using templates and reusable repositories
- Began organizing mods into collections and interconnected projects
- Expanded the use of Content Patcher to reduce dependencies and improve Android compatibility
- Started migrating some projects from framework-based solutions to Content Patcher-only implementations
- Improved the workflow for creating, updating and maintaining multiple mods simultaneously
- Started treating mod descriptions, manifests and documentation as part of the development process
- Began building a more structured mod portfolio
- Started exploring multi-platform publishing for mods
- Expanded the use of AI beyond development into research, documentation, translation and project management
- Started using AI to help design and balance new gameplay mechanics
- Established a more systematic workflow for testing, releasing and updating mods
- Began developing larger mod collections instead of isolated projects
- Started thinking about mods as long-term products rather than one-time projects
- Developed reusable patterns for shops, gifts, letters, recipes and progression systems
- Started creating projects designed from the beginning with Android compatibility in mind
- Began exploring ways to make mod development more scalable and repeatable

## 2025
- Learned the fundamentals of LLMs and generative AI
- Started using AI chat applications as development assistants
- Began experimenting with AI for programming and problem solving
- Discovered the potential of AI for accelerating creative and technical work
- Reframed the childhood dream of “being a bum and rich” into the goal of living off passive income
